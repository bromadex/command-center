How to use flips

1. Add a flip with quantity and costs. It will auto-create a ledger expense.
2. Click "Record Sale" on the flip card when you sell units.
3. The sale auto-creates a ledger income and calculates true profit based on average unit cost.

Settings

Backup, restore and data safety

savings Starting balance

Money you already had before you started using this app. It is added to your available balance on top of whatever your logged income and expenses add up to.

backup Back up everything

One JSON file with your ledger, debts, budgets, flips, Shein earnings, targets, ideas, study plan, books and notes, plus the Study Center. Data lives only in this browser, so export regularly. It does not sync between devices.

school Study Center data

Exams, CODESYS lessons, reading logs and to-dos are kept under their own storage key. They are included in the full export above; these buttons back up the Study Center on its own.

warning Danger zone
